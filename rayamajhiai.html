Dear Students of KMC-HSSD,

Education, research, innovation, and technology are rapidly transforming in the age of Artificial Intelligence (AI). To support your academic learning and research activities, RayamajhiAI can be used as an academic research assistant for research writing, report writing, thesis writing, literature review, data interpretation, academic writing, and related scholarly activities. You are encouraged to use RayamajhiAI to learn, explore, organize, improve, and critically develop your own academic work.
You can use RayamajhiAI for: Developing and refining research topics and titles Identifying research problems, questions, objectives, and hypotheses Planning research methodology Understanding research designs and sampling techniques Organizing and synthesizing literature reviews Identifying possible research gaps Developing questionnaires and interview guidelines Improving academic language, grammar, structure, and clarity Organizing research reports and thesis chapters Understanding and interpreting statistical results Developing tables, explanations, and discussion points Checking APA 7th citation and reference formats Preparing presentations, research proposals, and academic reports Generating ideas for research, innovation, and interdisciplinary studies
Remember: AI is an Assistant, Not a Replacement for You RayamajhiAI can help you think better, learn faster, and organize your academic work, but it cannot replace your responsibility as a student and researcher.
You must: Think → Research → Use AI → Verify → Analyze → Understand → Develop → Cite → Submit Do not simply: Generate → Copy → Paste → Submit
Academic Integrity is Essential Students must never use AI to: * Fabricate or manipulate research data * Invent references, quotations, authors, or research findings * Copy another person's work * Produce a complete thesis/report without understanding it * Misrepresent AI-generated material as independently verified research * Change statistical results to obtain a desired conclusion
Always verify important information using original research articles, books, official reports, datasets, and reliable academic sources. Our Goal The purpose of RayamajhiAI is not to make students dependent on AI. Its purpose is to help students become better learners, researchers, critical thinkers, writers, innovators, and problem solvers. Therefore, use RayamajhiAI responsibly, ethically, critically, and creatively for your academic journey. Education → Knowledge → Research → Evidence → Innovation → Solutions → Impact → Future Best wishes for your learning, research, innovation, and academic success.
