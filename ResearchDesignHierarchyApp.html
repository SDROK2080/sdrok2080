Key Principle

मुख्य सिद्धान्त

Each step builds on the previous one to ensure a strong and meaningful study. There must be logical coherence from your research question through to your final conclusions. This alignment creates a defensible logic that makes your research credible and trustworthy.

प्रत्येक चरण पूर्वको चरणमा निर्मित हुन्छ एक मजबूत र अर्थपूर्ण अध्ययन सुनिश्चित गर्न। आपको अनुसन्धान प्रश्न देखि अन्तिम निष्कर्ष सम्मको तार्किक सुसंगतता हुनुपर्छ।
