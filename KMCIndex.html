Who we are

A Legacy of Education in the Land of the Buddha

Kapilvastu Multiple Campus (KMC) stands as a pillar of education in Kapilvastu district, Lumbini Province, Nepal. Established in 2037 BS (1980 AD), our journey began with a vision to bridge the gap in higher education opportunities within the region.

Nestled in Taulihawa, approximately 500 meters southeast of Tilaurakot, the sacred birthplace of Lord Buddha, KMC carries a unique legacy.

Academics

Majors, Minors & Graduate Programs

KMC became the first institution in the region to offer Master's level programs. In 2069 BS (2012 AD), we received approval from Tribhuvan University to launch a Master of Education (M.Ed.) program, followed by the Master of Business Studies (MBS) program in 2073 BS (2016 AD).

Master of Business Studies (MBS)

A rigorous two-year program designed to equip students with the theoretical knowledge and practical skills necessary to excel in the dynamic world of business. Core courses cover fundamental business principles, followed by specializations in finance, marketing, human resource management, and operations management.

Since 2073 BS · TU Affiliated

Master of Education (M.Ed.)

Approved by Tribhuvan University in 2069 BS (2012 AD), the M.Ed. program provides local students the opportunity to pursue postgraduate studies in education without leaving the region.

Since 2069 BS · TU Affiliated

Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS)

A four-year undergraduate program covering management, accounting, economics, and business law, preparing students for careers in business and entrepreneurship.

TU Affiliated

Bachelor of Education (B.Ed.)

A comprehensive teacher-education program focused on pedagogy, educational psychology, and subject specialization.

TU Affiliated

Bachelor of Arts (BA)

A flexible humanities and social sciences program with majors in Nepali, English, sociology, political science, and more.

TU Affiliated

+2 Science / Management / Humanities

Two-year intermediate programs under the National Examinations Board (NEB), providing a strong foundation for further study.

NEB Affiliated
