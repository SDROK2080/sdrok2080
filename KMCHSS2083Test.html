📍 About The Department

The Department of Humanity & Social Science at Kapilvastu Multiple Campus is committed to academic excellence and holistic student development. Located in the historic region of Taulihawa — the birthplace of Lord Buddha — our department offers a rich academic environment with comprehensive B.A. programs across four years (2,000 marks total) under Tribhuvan University curriculum.

🎯 Mission

To foster critical thinking, research skills, and a deep understanding of human society, culture, and development through quality higher education.

📍 Location Advantage

Situated in ancient Kapilvastu — birthplace of Lord Buddha — providing unparalleled access to archaeological and cultural heritage study sites.

📚 Comprehensive Curriculum

Four-year B.A. program covering Sociology, Economics, History, Political Science, English, Nepali, Anthropology, and Rural Development Studies.

🌐 Digital Learning

Supported by UCDL Academy's bilingual (English/Nepali) digital platform — SDROK — making quality education accessible beyond campus boundaries.
