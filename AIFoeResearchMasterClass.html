⚖️ Ethics & Integrity Framework

Responsible AI use is non-negotiable in academic research

1

🔍 Transparency

Always disclose AI usage in methods and acknowledgments. Your readers deserve to know how research was conducted.

2

✅ Verification

Never submit AI output without human validation. AI assists; humans verify. Every claim must be checked.

3

💡 Originality

AI assists; humans create. You are the author. AI is your research assistant, not your ghostwriter.

4

🔒 Privacy

Never upload sensitive or proprietary data to public AI tools. Use institutional or private instances for confidential research.

5

👤 Accountability

The researcher is responsible for all outputs. You own the results, the analysis, and the conclusions.
