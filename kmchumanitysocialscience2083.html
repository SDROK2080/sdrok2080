About The Department

The Department of Humanity & Social Science at Kapilvastu Multiple Campus is committed to academic excellence and the holistic development of students. Located in the historic region of Taulihawa, Kapilvastu — the birthplace of Lord Buddha.

We offer comprehensive Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) programs across four years, designed to foster critical thinking, research skills, and a deep understanding of human society, culture, and development.
