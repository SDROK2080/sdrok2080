About The Department

The Department of Humanity & Social Science at Kapilvastu Multiple Campus is committed to academic excellence and holistic student development. Located in the historic region of Taulihawa, Kapilvastu — the birthplace of Lord Buddha — our department offers a rich academic environment.

We offer comprehensive Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) programs across four years (2,000 marks total), designed to foster critical thinking, research skills, and a deep understanding of human society, culture, and development under Tribhuvan University curriculum.

📌 Syllabus Standards

All syllabi follow Tribhuvan University standards with emphasis on:

  • Critical thinking and analytical skills development
  • Research methodology and academic writing
  • Contextual understanding of Nepali society and global perspectives
  • Practical application of theoretical concepts

✅ Exam Preparation Tips

  • 10-Year Trend Analysis: Identify frequently asked topics and question types
  • Marks Distribution: Note the weightage of different topics (Theory 70%, Practical/Essay 30%)
  • Sample Answers: Study model answers and marking schemes
  • Time Management: Practice previous papers within time limits
  • Contact Faculty: Reach out to department tutors for doubt clearance
