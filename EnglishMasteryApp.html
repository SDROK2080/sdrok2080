Daily Practice — 1 Hour Plan

10 min · Grammar

One focused grammar topic per day — review rules, do 5–10 exercises, note your errors and revisit them the next day.

⏱ 10 minutes Watch Practice

10 min · Vocabulary

Learn 5 new words — definition, pronunciation, example sentence, collocations. Write them in a vocabulary journal.

⏱ 10 minutes Watch Practice

10 min · Pronunciation

Repeat after native speakers — focus on one sound, stress pattern or intonation feature. Use a mirror to observe mouth shape.

⏱ 10 minutes Watch Resource

15 min · Listen to a Lecture or TED Talk

Choose one academic lecture or TED Talk — take notes on vocabulary, argument structure and speaker techniques. Summarise in 3 sentences.

⏱ 15 minutes TED Talks ted.com

15 min · Record a Short Speech

Pick any topic, speak for 1–3 minutes, record yourself, then self-evaluate on fluency, vocabulary, clarity & confidence. Track weekly progress.

⏱ 15 minutes Watch Tips

📅 Total: 60 Minutes of Focused Daily Practice

Consistency is the key to mastery. Even 30 minutes daily beats 3 hours once a week. Track your progress, record yourself regularly, and celebrate every small win. Repeat. Improve. Excel.
