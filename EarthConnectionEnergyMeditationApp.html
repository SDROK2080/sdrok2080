🔬 Scientific Evidence & Research

All wellness practices have different levels of scientific support. Here's what the research shows.

Understanding Evidence Levels

🟢 STRONG EVIDENCE
Proven by multiple peer-reviewed studies & clinical trials
Practices:
  • ✅ Meditation & Mindfulness
  • ✅ Yoga
  • ✅ Exercise & Physical Activity
  • ✅ Tai Chi & Qigong
  • ✅ Nature Exposure (120 min/week)
  • ✅ Sleep & Rest
🟡 EMERGING EVIDENCE
Promising research, but not yet conclusive
Practices:
  • ⭐ Forest Bathing (Phytoncides)
  • ⭐ Reiki
  • ⭐ Sound Therapy
  • ⭐ Pranic Healing
  • ⭐ Crystal Therapy (minimal evidence)
🔴 NOT ESTABLISHED
Lack of scientific evidence
Practices:
  • ❌ Aura Diagnosis
  • ❌ Crystal Healing (for disease)
  • ❌ Energy Field Manipulation (as medical treatment)
  • ❌ Guaranteed "Cures" via Energy
