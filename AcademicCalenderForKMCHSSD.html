💡 Why SMART? The SMART framework ensures every activity has clear goals, measurable targets, realistic strategies, direct alignment with institutional mission, and specific deadlines. This creates accountability at every level and helps us track progress systematically.

🎯 SMART Framework Explained

Every activity in this calendar follows the SMART framework. Here's what each letter means in the context of KMC-HSSD:

S – SPECIFIC Clear, defined outcome. What exactly will be accomplished? Example: "Complete 95% of course syllabus" rather than "teach well."
M – MEASURABLE Quantifiable target. How will we know we succeeded? Example: "80% student attendance," "90% assignment submission," "≥7/10 average assignment quality."
A – ACHIEVABLE Realistic goal with resources and support available. Not impossible; faculty have tools, training, and time. Example: "Research proposal by Dec 31 with mentoring support."
R – RELEVANT Aligned with TU curriculum standards and departmental mission (Quality, Research, Innovation, Digital transformation, Critical thinking, Social responsibility, Employability, Community engagement).
T – TIME-BOUND Exact dates and deadlines. Example: "Mid-term test: Nov 18-28, 2026" — not "sometime in November."

✅ Continuous Assessment Framework

Continuous assessment involves 7 components totaling 100% of the internal grade. No single test determines success; instead, multiple touchpoints track learning throughout the year.

Assessment Philosophy at KMC-HSSD: Assessment is not punishment—it is feedback. Every assessment activity gives students data about their learning progress and guides faculty on what to reteach or reinforce.

7 Assessment Components (Total = 100%)

Component Weight Timing & Method Success Target & Remarks
Attendance & Participation 10% Ongoing from Sep 18, 2026. Tracked in class register. Faculty mark present/absent daily. Target: ≥80% attendance. Remark: 3+ absences per month = automatic intervention
Assignments 15% 4 assignments throughout year. Due dates: Sep 25, Oct 15, Nov 25, Feb 15. Each worth 10 points. Target: ≥90% submission, 80% avg ≥7/10. Remark: Late submission: -20% penalty
Student Presentations 10% Two rounds: Nov 1-15 & Feb 1-15. Peer + faculty evaluation. 5-min presentation per student. Target: 100% participation, avg ≥6/10. Remark: Improves communication skills
Mid-Term Internal Test 20% Scheduled: Nov 18-28, 2026. 100-point exam. Pass score: 40/100. Faculty design; TU template optional. Target: ≥80% pass rate. Remark: Results used for remedial planning
Project / Field Work 15% Oct 1 – Mar 15. Dept-specific projects (e.g., Nepali: language project; History: field documentation). Faculty guide. Target: 100% submission, 70% avg score. Remark: Practical application of learning
Research Activity 15% Oct-Dec (proposal phase), Jan-Mar (data collection & analysis). 8 milestones tracked. Proposal due Dec 31. Target: ≥85% participation, ≥70% proposal score. Remark: Core for B.A. level
Final Internal Assessment 15% Mar 15-31. Faculty review all components and assign final internal grade. Submitted to TU by Mar 25. Target: 100% submission on time. Remark: Grades are final; no appeals after Mar 31

How to Support Students' Success

  • Early Intervention: If a student misses 3+ classes, contact them immediately
  • Mid-Term Review: After mid-term test, provide one-on-one feedback to low performers
  • Assignment Support: Conduct office hours before deadlines; allow questions
  • Research Mentoring: Monthly meetings with research coordinator; provide rubrics upfront
  • Grade Transparency: Share grade rubrics and expectations on day 1 of class
  • Celebrate Progress: Recognize students who improve, not just top performers

🔬 Research Development (8 Milestones)

Research is central to B.A. studies at KMC-HSSD. Every student conducts a small research project with faculty mentoring. Eight structured milestones ensure quality and timely completion.

Research Goals

  • Develop critical thinking & inquiry skills
  • Apply classroom learning to real-world problems
  • Build research literacy & data collection skills
  • Practice academic writing & presentation
  • Contribute to local knowledge & social understanding

8 Research Milestones & Deliverables

Milestone 1: Interest Exploration
Sep 18 – Oct 5, 2026
Deliverable: Topic Interest Sheet (1 page) — Student brainstorms 3-5 possible research topics related to their discipline. Faculty feedback provided.
Milestone 2: Topic Finalization
Oct 6-20, 2026
Deliverable: Topic Proposal (½ page) — Student selects ONE topic with research question. Faculty approves. Topics must be feasible & local-relevant.
Milestone 3: Literature Review Workshop
Oct 21 – Nov 15, 2026
Deliverable: Annotated Bibliography (15-20 sources) — Student finds & summarizes sources. Research Coordinator conducts library orientation. All students present findings.
Milestone 4: Methodology Training
Nov 5-15, 2026
Deliverable: Ethics Agreement + Methodology Quiz (pass ≥60/100) — Faculty teaches research methods appropriate to discipline (interview, survey, archival, etc.). Students sign ethics pledge.
Milestone 5: Proposal Development
Nov 16 – Dec 31, 2026
Deliverable: Research Proposal (3-5 pages) — Full proposal including research question, objectives, methodology, timeline, and expected outcomes. Target: 90% submission by Dec 31.
Milestone 6: Data Collection
Jan 1 – Feb 28, 2027
Deliverable: Monthly Progress Reports (1 page each) — Student logs data collected, challenges faced, and next steps. Faculty supervisor reviews and provides feedback. Target: 100% submission.
Milestone 7: Data Analysis & Writing
Feb 1-28, 2027 (Overlaps with data collection)
Deliverable: Analysis Report (10-15 pages) — Student analyzes collected data using appropriate methods and writes findings. Faculty provides 2-3 rounds of feedback. Target: ≥70% quality score.
Milestone 8: Presentation & Seminar
Mar 1-30, 2027
Deliverables: Slides + Handout (2 pages) + Peer Feedback Form — Students present findings to department. 15-minute presentation + 5-min Q&A. Target: 100% presentation, feedback forms returned.
🎯 Research Coordinator Role: Organizes workshops, tracks deadlines, provides LMS resources (templates, rubrics, sample papers), coordinates office hours with faculty, and ensures 100% milestone tracking.
