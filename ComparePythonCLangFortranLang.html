Learning outcomes
  • Explain the syntax and programming paradigms of Python, C, and Fortran.
  • Compare language features, memory models, execution characteristics, and application domains.
  • Implement equivalent algorithms in all three languages.
  • Evaluate trade-offs among readability, development speed, performance, and maintainability.
  • Apply appropriate languages and libraries to scientific and engineering problems.
  • Interpret benchmarking results with awareness of compilers, optimization, and hardware.
  • Develop foundational skills for HPC, numerical methods, and computational research.
