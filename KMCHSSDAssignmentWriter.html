TU FoHSS note

This app follows the Tribhuvan University cover-page style (University → Faculty → Campus). I could not find one official TU FoHSS assignment template online, so the section layout comes from your KMC-HSSD guide. Always follow your subject teacher's or the Faculty's written instructions if they differ.

Principles

Learn → Read → Think → Research → Analyze → Write → Reference → Reflect

Show your own understanding. Interpret and analyze; do not copy from books or websites. Relate the topic to Taulihawa, Kapilvastu or your own community.
