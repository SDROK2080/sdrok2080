Generate Evidence-Based Understanding

The RESEARCH pillar emphasizes structured research development from problem identification through rigorous academic writing. All B.A. students engage in original research as a core learning outcome.

📋 8-Milestone Research Cycle

  1. Idea Generation – Personal & departmental interests
  2. Problem Identification – Define research gap & significance
  3. Literature Review – Comprehensive scholarship review
  4. Question Formulation – Clear research questions & hypotheses
  5. Method Design – Research design & data collection
  6. Data Collection – Empirical or textual data gathering
  7. Analysis & Interpretation – Critical analysis & synthesis
  8. Findings Presentation – Written thesis, poster, or oral presentation

Create New Possibilities & Solutions

The INNOVATE pillar leverages AI-assisted learning, digital humanities, creative problem-solving, technology integration, and entrepreneurial thinking to transform academic work.

🤖 AI & Digital Tools for Learning

Students use approved tools responsibly:

  • Literature Discovery: Zotero, Mendeley, Google Scholar
  • Qualitative Analysis: NVivo, ATLAS.ti, MAXQDA
  • Quantitative Analysis: SPSS, Jamovi, R, Python
  • Data Visualization: Power BI, Tableau, Canva
  • Generative AI: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (with transparency)
  • Digital Humanities: QGIS mapping, digital archives
  • Writing Support: Grammarly, Turnitin
AI Policy: "AI tools should support learning and research, not replace students' independent academic work. All use of generative AI must be transparently disclosed and cited."
