Click **chart/graph icon** to see automatic summaries 3. Google Forms shows: - Average scores for scale questions - Frequency distribution for multiple choice - Word clouds for text responses ### Advanced Analysis (Using Google Sheets) 1. In Responses tab, click **"Create Spreadsheet"** 2. Google automatically links responses 3. Use formulas to calculate: - **Average by section:** `=AVERAGE(B2:B100)` (where B column = Q1-Q7) - **Category scores:** Sum section averages - **Percentiles:** Compare student against class - **Trend analysis:** Month-to-month comparison ### Recommended Calculations #### Individual Student Score: ``` Section 1 Score = (Q1+Q3+Q4+Q5) ÷ 4 × 25 (out of 100) Section 2 Score = (Q8+Q9+Q10+Q11+Q12+Q13+Q14) ÷ 7 × 25 Section 3 Score = (Q15+Q16+Q17+Q18+Q19+Q20) ÷ 6 × 25 Section 4 Score = (Q21+Q22+Q23+Q24+Q25) ÷ 5 × 25 Overall Score = Sum of all section scores Reverse Scoring for Q34 & Q49: 6 - original response (so 5 becomes 1, 1 becomes 5) ``` #### Class Benchmarking: ``` Average by section across all students Identify areas where >50% of students score <3 Flag students at risk (overall score <50%) Identify high performers (score >80%) ``` --- ## GOOGLE CLASSROOM INTEGRATION ### Option 1: Create as Classroom Assignment 1. Responses tab > Create spreadsheet 2. Download as .xlsx (right-click column > Download) 3. Use Excel formulas for statistical analysis 4. Create pivot tables for cross-tabulation ### Q: Need to send reminder emails **A:** - Google Forms doesn't auto-remind - Use Classroom announcement instead - Or use Google Apps Script (requires setup) - Manual email reminder (recommended for small class) --- ## IMPLEMENTATION TIMELINE **Week 1: Setup** - Create form in Google Forms - Add all 50 questions - Configure settings - Test with 2-3 sample responses **Week 2: Pilot Test** -
